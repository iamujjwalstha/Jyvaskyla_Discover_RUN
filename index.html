FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need to be athletic?

No. The format is built around movement, exploration, and team interaction rather than running performance. If you can comfortably move around the city, you can join.

Can I come alone?

Yes. In fact, many people will. The event is designed so joining solo still feels natural, social, and easy from the beginning.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, and your phone. The starter kit and route materials are provided on the day.

What if the weather changes?

The event is planned with weather in mind. If conditions change significantly, participants will be informed in advance through official channels.

Is this only for JAMK exchange students?

The event is designed specifically for incoming JAMK exchange students, which is what gives it a focused and welcoming atmosphere.

Why is it worth €15?

Because the ticket covers the full experience: starter kit, route materials, challenges, event atmosphere, and the closing social element — all packaged as a first-week activity.
