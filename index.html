What is it?

A city adventure built for your first week.

Jyväskylä Discovery Run is a one-day team event for incoming JAMK exchange students. You get placed in a small group, handed a city map, and set off to discover Jyväskylä through fun challenges at iconic locations.

No athletic ability required. No need to know anyone beforehand. Just show up, get matched with a team, and start exploring. The day ends with a campfire and music by the lake — the perfect first memory of your semester abroad.

Questions?

We've got answers.

Q Do I need to be athletic or a runner?

Not at all. The event is a mix of walking and light jogging. The focus is on exploring and completing challenges — not on speed. Everyone can participate comfortably.

Q Can I join alone if I don't know anyone?

Absolutely, and most people do. You'll be placed in a team of up to 6 at the start of the event. By the time you finish the first stop, you'll already have friends.

Q Is it only for JAMK exchange students?

The event is designed specifically for incoming exchange students at JAMK University of Applied Sciences for the Fall 2026 semester. If you're unsure whether you qualify, reach out on Instagram.

Q What is included in the €15 ticket?

Everything: your starter kit (water, snacks, sunscreen), city map, all challenges, team prizes, and the campfire closing event with food and music. No hidden costs.

Q What should I bring on the day?

Just comfortable shoes and weather-appropriate clothing. Everything else is provided. We recommend bringing a phone with a camera and a positive attitude.

Q What if the weather is bad?

The event takes place rain or shine with a backup plan in place for extreme conditions. Finland in August is usually mild — but we're prepared either way. You will be informed in advance if anything changes.

Q Can I sign up as a group with friends?

Yes! You can come with friends and register individually. Let the organizers know on the day if you'd like to be placed in the same team — we'll do our best to accommodate this.

Q When does registration close?

Registration closes one day before the event (20 August) or earlier if all 60 spots are filled. Registration is through Kide.app — link above. We strongly recommend registering early.
